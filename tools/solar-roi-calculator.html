    How It Works

    This 25-year financial model simulates the real-world economics of rooftop solar. Here is the calculation logic:

    1. Gross cost: System size (kW) × 1,000 × cost per watt = total installed price before incentives.
    2. Net cost: Gross cost minus federal tax credit and any state rebate you enter.
    3. Annual production: System size × daily solar resource (kWh/m²/day) × 365 days × 0.75 system-loss factor. Degradation is modeled at 0.5% per year.
    4. Self-consumption vs. export: Production up to your annual usage is valued at the full retail rate. Excess production is credited at the net-metering rate you select.
    5. Escalation: Utility rates rise by your chosen annual percentage, which increases the value of every kilowatt-hour you avoid buying.
    6. Payback: The year when cumulative savings exceed the net system cost. If it never happens within 25 years, we report '>25'.

    Tip: Use the Home Energy Bill Breakdown tool first to get your accurate annual usage. Oversizing solar relative to usage wastes money.
