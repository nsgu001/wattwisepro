    How It Works

    This analyzer compares your current HVAC efficiency against a potential upgrade using engineering formulas accepted by the Air-Conditioning, Heating, and Refrigeration Institute (AHRI). Here is the methodology:

    1. System sizing: We estimate tonnage from your square footage using the rule of thumb 1 ton per 500 sq ft (adjusted for climate).
    2. Cooling load hours: Each DOE climate zone has an approximate annual full-load equivalent hour count, ranging from 300 (Zone 7) to 2,800 (Zone 1).
    3. Annual kWh: (Tons × 12,000 BTU/hr × load hours) ÷ (SEER × 1,000). This is the standard SEER-to-kWh conversion.
    4. Heat pump bonus: If you select heat pump, we estimate HSPF as 0.7 × SEER and add heating-season kWh using the same formula with heating load hours.
    5. Payback: Upgrade cost ÷ annual dollar savings. We do not model utility inflation here, so the payback is conservative.

    Tip: Seal air leaks and add insulation before upgrading equipment. A smaller, more efficient system will often outperform an oversized high-SEER unit in a leaky house.
